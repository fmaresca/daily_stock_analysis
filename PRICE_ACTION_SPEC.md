## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This specification defines the architecture, data contracts, mathematical rules, and visualization standards for adding an **Al Brooks Price Action Analysis Module** with interactive candlestick charting to DeltaHarvest.

The implementation strictly follows the published concepts of Al Brooks (author of *Reading Price Charts Bar by Bar* and the *Trading Price Action* trilogy, John Wiley & Sons), parameterized into deterministic, pure TypeScript functions without reproducing copyrighted text.

### Architectural Principles
1. **Additive-Only:** Zero modifications to existing screener rituals (Steps 1–7), Tax Alpha engines, Ask Strategy Agent, or existing chart components (`InteractiveChart.tsx`). All price action code lives in dedicated directories (`web/src/priceAction/` and `web/src/components/priceAction/`).
2. **Deterministic & Non-Repainting:** Swing pivots and pattern confirmations require an $N$-bar confirmation delay ($N=2$ bars) to ensure historical markers do not repaint.
3. **Pure Functions:** The detection engine is 100% side-effect free, testable in standard Node.js/Vitest test runners without DOM or network dependencies.
4. **Transparent Rationale:** Every flagged setup outputs its exact detection parameters, numerical thresholds, and human-readable explanation alongside an explicit confidence/uncertainty note.

### 2.2 Pipeline Granularity & Update Cadence

1. **Daily Bars (Primary Horizon):**
   - Active default across DeltaHarvest.
   - Provides 120 to 252 trading days of historical data (6 months to 1 year).
   - High data integrity, reliable post-close settlement, no intraday session alignment or timezone jitter.
2. **Intraday Bars (Deferred Horizon):**
   - Tradier supports `interval=15min` or `interval=5min`; Yahoo supports `interval=5m`.
   - **Recommendation for v1:** Support **Daily Bars** first. The canonical bar pipeline interface (`priceAction/bars.ts`) will accept an ISO 8601 timestamp string or UNIX epoch seconds, ensuring seamless addition of intraday bars without breaking downstream consumers.

### 2.3 Data Pipeline Normalization & Policies (`priceAction/bars.ts`)

To feed the detection engine, incoming raw bar arrays must be normalized into a canonical series:
```typescript
export interface CanonicalBar {
  time: string;       // YYYY-MM-DD for daily, ISO 8601 for intraday
  timestamp: number;  // UNIX epoch in seconds
  open: number;
  high: number;
  low: number;
  close: number;
  volume: number;
}
```

#### Documented Edge-Case Policies:
1. **Weekend & Holiday Gaps:** The market calendar naturally omits weekends and NYSE market holidays. **Policy:** Never interpolate artificial bars. Gaps represent closed market sessions. Indicators that require $N$ periods operate over $N$ trading bars, not calendar days.
2. **Bad Ticks:** Any bar where `high < low`, or `high < max(open, close)`, or `low > min(open, close)`, or any price is $\le 0$ or `NaN`. **Policy:** Bar is rejected and logged. If bad ticks exceed 2% of the series, the series is flagged as invalid.
3. **Missing Bar Data (Zero Volume / Flat Bar):** Occasional illiquid tickers report zero volume or flat price. **Policy:** Retain flat bar only if open == high == low == close; volume set to 0.
4. **Sorting:** Incoming bars must be sorted in strictly ascending chronological order (`bar[i].timestamp < bar[i+1].timestamp`). Duplicate timestamps are deduplicated by taking the latest tick.

### 4.1 Evaluation of `cm45t3r/candlestick` vs. Native TypeScript Engine

The `candlestick` npm package was evaluated for use as the bar-classification layer:
- **Pros:** Zero runtime dependencies, TypeScript definitions, handles classic patterns (doji, hammer, engulfing).
- **Cons & Gap:** Brooks price action is fundamentally different from Japanese candlestick patterns. In Brooks' methodology:
  1. A "Bull Trend Bar" is strictly defined by body-to-range ratio and close location in the top third of the total bar range, not whether it is a white candle or engulfing candle.
  2. Signal bars require context: a bull bar is only a valid "Signal Bar" if it occurs at a pullback low or breakout point.
  3. Pullback counting (H1/H2, L1/L2) requires sequential bar-by-bar state tracking across multiple swings.
- **Architectural Decision:** Implement the bar classifiers as pure, zero-dependency TypeScript functions in `priceAction/detect.ts` using a consolidated `PriceActionConfig` interface. This eliminates external package drift, ensures exact alignment with Brooks' thresholds, and keeps the bundle lightweight.

### 5.1 Bar Classification Rules

Let $H_t, L_t, O_t, C_t$ denote the High, Low, Open, and Close of bar $t$.  
Let $R_t = H_t - L_t$ (total range) and $B_t = |C_t - O_t|$ (body size).

| Classification | Parameterized Detection Rule | Open-Source Equivalent | Known Ambiguities & Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bull Trend Bar** | 1. $C_t > O_t$<br>2. $\frac{B_t}{R_t} \ge \text{trendBodyMinRatio}$ (0.50)<br>3. $\frac{C_t - L_t}{R_t} \ge \text{bullCloseThirdRatio}$ (0.67) | Sierra Chart `TrendBarType == BULL`<br>`ck-2027` Bar Reading 01 | Small upper tail is acceptable if close remains in upper third. Highly volatile bars with wide wicks require tail check. |
| **Bear Trend Bar** | 1. $C_t < O_t$<br>2. $\frac{B_t}{R_t} \ge \text{trendBodyMinRatio}$ (0.50)<br>3. $\frac{C_t - L_t}{R_t} \le \text{bearCloseThirdRatio}$ (0.33) | Sierra Chart `TrendBarType == BEAR`<br>`ck-2027` Bar Reading 01 | Small lower tail is acceptable if close remains in bottom third. |
| **Doji / Range Bar** | 1. $\frac{B_t}{R_t} \le \text{dojiBodyMaxRatio}$ (0.25), OR<br>2. $\text{bearCloseThirdRatio} < \frac{C_t - L_t}{R_t} < \text{bullCloseThirdRatio}$ | Sierra Chart `DojiBar`<br>`candlestick` Doji | Traders differ on whether close in the middle third with moderate body is a doji or weak trend bar; classified as Range Bar. |
| **Inside Bar (IB)** | 1. $H_t \le H_{t-1}$<br>2. $L_t \ge L_{t-1}$ | Sierra Chart `InsideBar`<br>`candlestick` Harami variant | Equal highs or lows are treated as inside bars if opposite boundary is strictly contained. |
| **Outside Bar (OB)** | 1. $H_t \ge H_{t-1}$<br>2. $L_t \le L_{t-1}$ | Sierra Chart `OutsideBar`<br>`candlestick` Engulfing variant | An outside bar creates volatility expansion; direction is determined by whether $C_t$ closes in top or bottom third. |
| **Bull Signal Bar** | 1. Bull Trend Bar, OR bar with lower tail $\ge \text{tailDominanceRatio}$ (0.33) and close in upper half.<br>2. Occurs at support (EMA20, range bottom, or pullback low). | Sierra Chart `SignalBarBull`<br>`ck-2027` With-Trend 03 | Signal bar requires follow-through (entry bar breaking above signal bar high) to trigger a valid entry. |
| **Bear Signal Bar** | 1. Bear Trend Bar, OR bar with upper tail $\ge \text{tailDominanceRatio}$ (0.33) and close in lower half.<br>2. Occurs at resistance (EMA20, range top, or pullback high). | Sierra Chart `SignalBarBear`<br>`ck-2027` With-Trend 03 | Signal bar requires entry bar breaking below signal bar low. |

### 5.2 Trend Identification & Swing Legs

#### Swing Pivot Rules (Non-Repainting)
A bar at index $p$ is confirmed as a **Swing High** if:
$$H_p > \max(H_{p-k}, \dots, H_{p-1}) \quad \text{and} \quad H_p > \max(H_{p+1}, \dots, H_{p+k})$$
where $k = \text{swingPivotLookback} = 2$.  
*Confirmation lag:* The pivot is confirmed at the close of bar $p + k$. It never repaints.

A bar at index $p$ is confirmed as a **Swing Low** if:
$$L_p < \min(L_{p-k}, \dots, L_{p-1}) \quad \text{and} \quad L_p < \min(L_{p+1}, \dots, L_{p+k})$$

#### Trend Leg Classification
- **Bull Trend Leg:** Sequence of confirmed swing pivots exhibiting:
  $$\text{High}_n > \text{High}_{n-1} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Low}_n > \text{Low}_{n-1} \quad (\text{Higher Highs, Higher Lows})$$
- **Bear Trend Leg:** Sequence of confirmed swing pivots exhibiting:
  $$\text{High}_n < \text{High}_{n-1} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Low}_n < \text{Low}_{n-1} \quad (\text{Lower Highs, Lower Lows})$$
- **Always-In State (AIL / AIS):**
  - **Always-In Long (AIL):** Triggered when price closes above a prior confirmed swing high with a strong Bull Trend Bar, or upon confirmed Bull Breakout / Major Trend Reversal.
  - **Always-In Short (AIS):** Triggered when price closes below a prior confirmed swing low with a strong Bear Trend Bar, or upon confirmed Bear Breakout / Major Trend Reversal.

### 5.3 Trading Range Detection

A sequence of $M$ bars ($M \ge \text{rangeMinBars} = 5$) is classified as a **Trading Range** when:
1. **Overlap Metric:** For each adjacent pair $(t, t-1)$, calculate overlap:
   $$\text{Overlap}_t = \max\left(0, \min(H_t, H_{t-1}) - \max(L_t, L_{t-1})\right)$$
   $$\text{OverlapRatio}_t = \frac{\text{Overlap}_t}{\min(R_t, R_{t-1})}$$
   At least $\text{rangeOverlapBarPct}$ (60%) of bars in the window have $\text{OverlapRatio}_t \ge \text{barOverlapMinRatio}$ (0.50).
2. **Alternating Closes:** Frequent alternation between bull and bear closes without consecutive new swing highs/lows.
3. **Range Boundaries:**
   $$\text{RangeTop} = \max_{i \in \text{range}}(H_i), \quad \text{RangeBottom} = \min_{i \in \text{range}}(L_i)$$

### 5.4 Setups & Bar-Sequence Patterns

| Setup Type | Brooks Concept | Exact Bar-Sequence Pattern | Invalidation Level | Measured-Move Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High 1 / High 2 (H1 / H2) Pullback** | With-trend pullback entry in bull trend | 1. Market is in Bull Trend or AIL.<br>2. Pullback starts (bars making lower highs/lows).<br>3. **H1:** First bar where $H_t > H_{t-1}$.<br>4. If H1 stalls and a secondary pullback occurs, the next bar where $H_j > H_{j-1}$ is **H2** (two-legged ABC correction).<br>5. Trigger: Bar $t+1$ breaks above High of Signal Bar. | $L_{\text{signal}} - 1 \text{ tick}$ | $\text{Target} = C_{\text{entry}} + (\text{High}_{\text{leg1}} - \text{Low}_{\text{leg1}})$ |
| **Low 1 / Low 2 (L1 / L2) Pullback** | With-trend pullback entry in bear trend | 1. Market is in Bear Trend or AIS.<br>2. Pullback starts (bars making higher lows/highs).<br>3. **L1:** First bar where $L_t < L_{t-1}$.<br>4. If L1 stalls and secondary leg pushes up, the next bar where $L_j < L_{j-1}$ is **L2**.<br>5. Trigger: Bar $t+1$ breaks below Low of Signal Bar. | $H_{\text{signal}} + 1 \text{ tick}$ | $\text{Target} = C_{\text{entry}} - (\text{High}_{\text{leg1}} - \text{Low}_{\text{leg1}})$ |
| **Breakout Entry (BO)** | Trend breakout from trading range | 1. Valid Trading Range established.<br>2. Strong Trend Bar closes completely outside RangeTop (bull) or RangeBottom (bear).<br>3. Follow-through bar continues in breakout direction. | Opposite side of Breakout Bar ($L_{\text{BO}}$ for bull, $H_{\text{BO}}$ for bear) | $\text{Range Height} = \text{RangeTop} - \text{RangeBottom}$<br>$\text{Target} = \text{RangeTop} + \text{Range Height}$ |
| **Failed Breakout Reversal (FBO)** | Range fade / failed breakout | 1. Bar penetrates RangeTop or RangeBottom by 1–3 bars.<br>2. Immediate strong reversal bar closes back inside range.<br>3. Entry on break of reversal bar extreme. | 1 tick beyond extreme of failed breakout wick | Opposite boundary of Trading Range ($\text{RangeBottom}$ for bull fail, $\text{RangeTop}$ for bear fail) |
| **Major Trend Reversal (MTR) / Double Top & Bottom** | 3-step trend reversal | 1. Trendline broken with strong counter-trend move.<br>2. Retest of previous swing extreme within $\text{mtrExtremeTolerancePct}$ (1.0%).<br>3. Strong opposite signal bar confirms reversal.<br>4. Double Top (bear MTR) or Double Bottom (bull MTR). | 1 tick beyond extreme of the retest swing high/low | Height of reversal formation projected from breakout neckline |

### 5.5 Measured-Move Projections Arithmetic

1. **Leg 1 = Leg 2 (Two-Legged Move):**
   $$\Delta_{\text{Leg1}} = \text{High}_{\text{Leg1}} - \text{Low}_{\text{Leg1}}$$
   $$\text{Bull Target} = \text{Low}_{\text{Pullback}} + \Delta_{\text{Leg1}}$$
   $$\text{Bear Target} = \text{High}_{\text{Pullback}} - \Delta_{\text{Leg1}}$$
2. **Trading Range Height Projection:**
   $$H_{\text{Range}} = \text{RangeTop} - \text{RangeBottom}$$
   $$\text{Bull Breakout Target} = \text{RangeTop} + H_{\text{Range}}$$
   $$\text{Bear Breakout Target} = \text{RangeBottom} - H_{\text{Range}}$$

## 7. Market Regime & SPX/NDX Proxy Logic (Prompt 7 Preview)

The engine runs over daily bars of broad market proxies (SPY/SPX and QQQ/NDX):
1. **Regime States:**
   - `BULL_TREND`: Series of HH/HL, Always-In Long.
   - `BEAR_TREND`: Series of LH/LL, Always-In Short.
   - `TRADING_RANGE`: Confirmed range box active, alternating bars.
2. **Agreement Matrix:**
   - **Agree:** Both proxies in same direction $\rightarrow$ Full setup confidence.
   - **Mixed / Conflict:** Proxies in different states (e.g. SPX in range, NDX in bear leg) $\rightarrow$ "Mixed market regime — reduce position sizing / wait for convergence."
   - Never forced into agreement.
